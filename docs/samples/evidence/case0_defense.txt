Defense filing - Acme Cloud Inc, case 0.

We ship no NOTICE file and no LICENSE file. Our parser module was
transcribed from libtinyparse and lightly reformatted; we do not claim
independent authorship. We do not distribute source and consider our
runtime a trade secret.

We understand libtinyparse is GPL-3.0. We do not believe that obligation
applies to our internal use, and we ask the tribunal to declare that
running the code inside a closed SaaS product is not "distribution".
