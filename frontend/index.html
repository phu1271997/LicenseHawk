§ III.a · Adjudicate

Convene the AI Tribunal Motion LH-3a

Once both bonds are posted, either party may request adjudication. Validators fetch both codebases and the defense evidence, re-read the license text, and rule. Expect one to two minutes for consensus.

§ III.b · Enforce

Enforce Verdict & Distribute Bonds Motion LH-3b

After adjudication, either party may call enforce. Bonds are distributed by the ruling: an infringing respondent forfeits its bond to the claimant; a cleared respondent recovers its bond and takes the claimant's as defense cost; an unclear case returns each bond to its poster; a retaliatory claim forfeits the claimant's entire bond.

§ III.c · Withdraw

Cancel an Unanswered Filing Motion LH-3c

If the named respondent never posts a bond, the claimant may withdraw the filing while it is still CASE_FILED and recover the full claimant bond. Once the case is contested this route closes.

How the Court works

LicenseHawk is a four-step, on-chain tribunal for open-source license claims. Every step lives on GenLayer's Studio Network; the AI Jury reads unstructured code and legal text directly from the web.

Consensus on meaning, not wording

Validators must agree on the verdict (INFRINGEMENT_CONFIRMED / DERIVATIVE_UNCLEAR / NO_INFRINGEMENT / RETALIATORY_CLAIM) and, for infringement rulings, the remedy. Prose reasoning may diverge. One-step tolerance is granted only around Derivative Unclear.

Remedies (Infringement only)

  1. RELEASE_SOURCE — respondent must publish corresponding source (GPL / AGPL).
  2. ADD_ATTRIBUTION — respondent must ship license text and notice (MIT / BSD / Apache).
  3. REMOVE_CODE — respondent must strip the infringing code.
  4. PAY_DAMAGES — a monetary remedy is warranted (rare).

Why on-chain?

Solidity cannot fetch raw source code from the web, cannot read a license document, and cannot exercise judgement on derivative works. Strip out the web reads and the LLM and there is no product left — this is why LicenseHawk lives on GenLayer.
